N40 ESP32 Extension Board

The N40 is an expansion and breakout board for compatible ESP32 and ESP32-S3 development boards. It brings the GPIO, power, enable, and communication pins to clearly labeled 2.54 mm headers and screw terminals, making it easier to connect external modules, prototype circuits, and measure signals.

Key Features​

  • Breaks out the ESP32/ESP32-S3 GPIO and power pins for convenient access
  • 2.54 mm pin headers for Dupont-wire connections
  • 2.54 mm-pitch KF screw terminals for direct wire connections
  • Dedicated 5 V and GND distribution headers
  • GPIO status LEDs for visual signal indication
  • TXS0108EPWR level-translation ICs with a specified data rate of up to 60 Mbps
  • 7-12 V DC input through a 5.5 x 2.1 mm barrel connector
  • Four 3 mm mounting holes

Compatible Core Boards​

The extension board is designed for compatible ESP32 and ESP32-S3 boards with the required header arrangement. The product specification lists the following core-board options:

  • ESP32-WROOM development board
  • ESP32-S3-WROOM development board
  • YD-ESP32-S3 N16R8 development board
Check the pinout before installation

ESP32 development boards are available with different dimensions and pin arrangements. Before applying power, verify that the core board matches the N40 socket spacing and that every 3.3 V, 5 V, GND, RST, TX, and RX pin is correctly aligned.

Board Overview​

The N40 provides three connection methods around the core-board sockets:

  1. Signal headers - Use 2.54 mm Dupont leads to access GPIO, communication, reset, and power pins.
  2. Power distribution headers - Use the dedicated 5 V and GND rows to power external sensors and modules.
  3. KF screw terminals - Insert stripped wires and tighten the terminal screws for a secure connection.

The GPIO LEDs are connected to the corresponding core-board signals and provide a visual indication when the logic state changes.

Power Supply​

Connect a regulated 7-12 V DC supply to the 5.5 x 2.1 mm barrel connector. A 12 V/2 A adapter is recommended.

Power connection

Confirm the adapter voltage, polarity, and core-board orientation before switching on the power. Do not connect an external supply directly to a GPIO pin.

Header Functions​

J7, J85 V distribution; pins 1-22 provide 5 V for external devices

Dimensions​

The extension board measures 87.00 x 80.00 x 12.00 mm. The mounting-hole center spacing is 80.20 x 73.20 mm, and the mounting holes accept 3 mm screws.
