W13 (Mesh Tracker DEVK1.0)

Product Overview​

Product Introduction:

The Mesh Tracker W13 is a LoRa self-organizing node hardware product series. It relies on LoRa radio to build a decentralized multi-hop Mesh network without base stations or cellular signals, enabling offline encrypted text messaging, GPS location sharing, and environmental sensor data collection.

This product adopts the mainstream nRF52840 chip + E22-900MM22S LoRa RF chip (based on the popular SX1262) + L76K multi-mode GPS (BeiDou/GPS/GLONASS) + BMP388 (pressure/temperature/altitude) sensor chip + SHT41 (temperature/humidity) sensor chip, and Bluetooth 5.0. It is designed with a TFT color backlight display, paired with LoRa and GPS modules. It integrates the WiFi, Bluetooth, and LoRa communication standards commonly used in IoT, supports the 850-930MHz frequency band and LoRaWAN protocol, is compatible with Mesh networks, and meets the needs of scenarios such as smart cities and industrial control.

Product Positioning:

This product is primarily targeted at IoT engineers, smart hardware engineers, and open-source hardware enthusiasts, providing them with a hardware development platform for WiFi, Bluetooth, and LoRa communication technologies. It is an extensible Mesh development hardware platform for makers/developers with high demands, focusing on offline self-organizing network communication without public internet;

This product is aimed at makers, hardware developers, and DIY enthusiasts; satisfying enthusiasts who want to modify the enclosure, attach external sensors, build gateways, or modify firmware through this product. It also targets college students and STEM education groups for wireless communication course experiments and course design. As well as hikers, mountaineers, trail runners, campers, and off-road vehicle teams; and emergency preparedness (Prepper) groups. This product features solar power and has built-in sensor chips, so it can be directly applied to research teams, small project integrators, field monitoring, search-and-rescue drills, and agricultural monitoring prototype validation; it is not intended for large-scale mass production, but rather for quickly building LoRa-Mesh prototypes and rapidly validating business logic.

Product Features​

Feature Overview​

The Mesh Tracker W13 board iteration (with TFT backlight color screen) is a low-power multi-protocol positioning and tracking terminal development board for IoT scenarios. It integrates high-precision positioning, environment/motion sensing, multi-mode wireless communication, and local TFT display functions. Adopting a miniaturized industrial design, it supports rapid development and flexible deployment, and can be widely applied to IoT scenarios such as asset tracking, device monitoring, personnel positioning, and smart logistics. It combines hardware extensibility with software ease of use, meeting the rapid deployment needs of individual developers and enterprise-level projects.

  • Multi-mode Positioning & Communication: Integrates multi-protocol wireless communication and positioning capabilities, balancing transmission distance, rate, and low power consumption to meet network requirements of different scenarios
  • TFT Color Local Display: Equipped with a small TFT color screen for real-time local display of device status, positioning data, and sensor values. No need to rely on external devices — visualization is more intuitive, with excellent readability under strong sunlight, and a great visual interaction experience for long text and simple map browsing.
  • Ultra-low Power Design: Supports multiple power consumption modes, with deep sleep power reaching the μA level, compatible with battery power supply, meeting the needs of outdoor/mobile scenarios with long battery life.
  • Miniaturized & Easy to Integrate: Compact industrial design, lightweight body, rich standardized interfaces, can be quickly integrated into various devices/assets, flexible deployment.
  • One-stop Development Experience: Compatible with mainstream development frameworks, provides rich development examples and documentation, lowers the development threshold, and supports rapid prototype validation and mass production.
  • Multi-sensor Integration: Built-in barometric + temperature/humidity environmental sensor chips, directly realizing multi-dimensional data collection of positioning + sensing, meeting scenario-based perception needs.
  • Industrial-grade Reliability: Wide-temperature operating design, strong anti-interference capability, suitable for complex usage environments such as industrial and outdoor.
  • Meshtastic Firmware: Compatible with Meshtastic firmware, enabling seamless setup.
  • Three Power Options: Supports Type-C fast charging, solar input, and lithium battery power supply, flexibly deployable in various environments — ideal for outdoor and mobile applications.
  • Flexible Display Support: Optional 1.54-inch TFT color and 2.13/1.54-inch e-ink screens, providing diverse display options to meet different power consumption and visual feedback needs.
  • High Extensibility: Features a Grove interface, includes PTH headers and SWD debug interface, supporting custom hardware expansion and advanced development.

The product adopts a card-type/small module industrial design, with the body integrating a TFT color display, function buttons, antennas, standardized interfaces, and indicator LEDs. The overall size is small, balancing portability and installation stability.

Product Characteristics​

  1. Uses Nordic nRF52840: ARM® Cortex-M4, with built-in hardware floating-point unit FPU, max frequency 64 MHz, built-in WiFi and BLE functionality;
  2. Integrates LoRa and GPS modules, easily meeting the needs of communication and data transmission in networkless environments, as well as outdoor positioning and tracking;
  3. On-board barometric and temperature/humidity chips, directly enabling environmental detection. Without external sensor interfaces, data pairing with wireless synchronization and upload can be intuitively implemented, especially suitable for small-scale outdoor field monitoring;
  4. On-board TFT color display, locally and in real time displaying core information such as device ID, network status, positioning data, sensor values, and battery level;
  5. Equipped with function buttons, enabling manual operations such as device power on/off, mode switching, data refresh, and pairing connection;
  6. Equipped with multiple power consumption modes (active / light sleep / deep sleep), switchable manually or remotely, adapting to different battery life needs;
  7. Integrates battery management and power monitoring functions, real-time collection of battery voltage / remaining power, with low-battery local alert + remote reporting;
  8. Equipped with status indicator LEDs, intuitively displaying device status such as power, communication, positioning, and faults;
  9. Provides rich standardized expansion interfaces, supporting external sensors, peripherals, and firmware upgrade/local debugging;
  10. Supports local firmware upgrade (USB) and remote upgrade (OTA), enabling functional iteration and issue fixes;
  11. Features local data caching: caches key data when communication is disconnected, automatically resumes transmission after recovery, ensuring data integrity.

01_w13_gps​

Program Introduction

This demo program verifies the positioning capability of the W13's on-board L76K GNSS module. The program receives NMEA sentences via UART, uses TinyGPSPlus to parse out positioning status, satellite count, latitude/longitude, altitude, speed, heading, and time/date, and refreshes them to the TFT color screen in real time.

Main features include:

  • Four-item power-on self-test: TFT (screen available), GPS UART (serial port receiving data), GPS NMEA (sentence parsing successful), PMTK CFG (configuration command sent). Each item independently displays OK / FAIL. The bottom hint changes from SELFTEST RUNNING to SELFTEST DONE.
  • Ten-line real-time data display: FIX / SAT / HDOP / LNG / LAT / ALT / SPD / CRS / TIME / DATE
  • Three-state positioning display: 3D FIX (positioned), SEARCH (searching for satellites), NO SIG (no signal). Latitude/longitude display -- when not positioned.
  • Beijing Time automatic conversion: NMEA original time is UTC. The program converts to UTC+8 and handles cross-day carryover. TIME / DATE directly display Beijing time.

Hardware Connection

  • Connect the board to the computer with a USB cable
  • Connect the GNSS antenna to the on-board IPEX connector, and place near a window or outdoors if possible (first positioning cold start takes 30s+) (connection shown below)

Performance

  • After entering the data page, SAT increases as satellites are found. Once positioned, FIX changes from SEARCH to 3D FIX.

After flashing the firmware, you can view the actual results.

02_w13_lora​

Program Introduction

This demo program verifies the RF transmit/receive capability of SX1262, covering three operating states: receive, transmit, and continuous carrier wave, as well as modulation mode switching, interrupt status viewing, and chip-level power management.

On power-on, it first runs an RF self-test (SPI communication, standby, chip version, RF parameters, device error, RF switch link — six items). Only when all are OK does it enter the main interface. If any item FAIL, it stays on the self-test page; press any key to rerun.

Main features include:

  • Four-module structure, switch with LEFT / RIGHT, header right side displays 1/4 ~ 4/4
  • Module 1 Transmit/Receive Control: UP → MODE:915TX (auto-send Hello n incrementing packet every 2s), DOWN → MODE:915RX (default on power-on), PRESS → MODE:CW continuous wave (press again to return to RX)
  • Module 2 Modulation Mode: UP / DOWN selects between LORA / FSK / GFSK, PRESS to enter; once inside, can also switch 915TX / 915RX, press PRESS again to return to the selection list
  • Module 3 Status & Interrupts: chip version number, packet type, device error code DERR, raw IRQ register, RX / TX / CRC / HERR / TMO five counters. PRESS clears statistics.
  • Module 4 Power Switch: PRESS cycles between ACTIVE → SLEEP → PARK → wake, demonstrating chip-level low power.

Hardware Connection

  • Connect the board to the computer with a USB cable
  • Connect the 915 MHz antenna to the on-board LoRa IPEX connector (do not transmit for long periods without an antenna — this can easily damage the power amplifier)

Performance

  • After all six self-test items are OK, it automatically enters Module 1, default MODE:915RX, RSSI / SNR change in real time with the environment.

  • Press UP to switch to MODE:915TX, Data displays Hello 1, Hello 2…, Cnt increments every 2s

  • Press PRESS to switch to MODE:CW, Data displays red CW ON, RSSI / SNR change to --

  • Enter modulation mode

  • Status & interrupts

  • Power switch

03_w13_base​

Program Introduction

This demo program is an MCU kernel-level self-test suite. It splits nRF52840's five basic capabilities — clock, non-volatile storage, wired communication, Bluetooth, and timer/PWM — into five modules, running them one by one and giving OK / FAIL conclusions.

Main features include:

  • Five-module summary page: CLOCK / FLASH / COMM / BLE / PWM five rows, each displaying passed count/total. All passed is green, any failure is red, not run is gray ----
  • RIGHT / LEFT to turn pages, PRESS to run the current module. Sub-items light up one by one with their results (more intuitive than whole-page transitions).
  • Module 1 CLOCK+POWER: HFCLK run confirmation, LFCLK alive, 10s timing accuracy (±100ms), DCDC regulator register read/write, WFI idle sleep, System OFF deep sleep + GPREGRET magic number preserved across deep sleep
  • Module 2 FLASH: Operates via SoftDevice's sd_flash_page_erase / sd_flash_write asynchronous interface (directly touching NRF_NVMC triggers an exception reset). Tests page erase to all 0xFF, write-back (0x00 / 0xA5 / incrementing three groups), erase-then-write characteristic, power-off retention persistent counter
  • Module 3 COMM WIRED: UARTLP (UART loopback), SPILP (SPI loopback), I2CSCAN (bus scan), USBECHO (TinyUSB CDC echo)
  • Module 4 BLE: STACK (protocol stack enabled), ADVERT (advertising started), CONN (connection established), ECHO (characteristic loopback)
  • Module 5 PWM/TIMER: TIMIRQ (timer interrupt period), PWMFREQ (PWM frequency measured), PWMDUTY (duty cycle sweep), BEEP (buzzer beep)
  • On power-on, runs a simplified self-test first (TFT / BLE / CLK three items)
  • Serial port synchronously outputs each item's judgment basis and measured values, making it easy to locate which level has the problem

05_w13_epd​

Program Introduction

This demo program verifies the driver, partial refresh, and low-power sleep of the 2.13" GDEY0213B74 e-ink display (SSD1680 driven). The screen's physical resolution is 122×250, the program uses it in landscape mode as 250×122; frame buffer uses double buffering (current frame + previous frame), partial refresh only pushes the changed strip.

LEFT / RIGHT to turn pages, each page full-screen refresh takes about 1.7s. On the REFRESH page, press UP / DOWN to change the counter value — only the progress bar changes. On the SLEEP page, press PRESS and the full screen goes black, after which only MENU can wake it.

Main features include:

  • INIT: Executes pin detection and reset timing, sends initialization sequence, displays measured time on screen; on power-on first probes panel type (after reset + SWRESET, if BUSY goes high within 300ms, it's determined to be an e-ink screen).
  • REFRESH: Compares two refresh methods — partial refresh (0x22=0xFC non-flash waveform) vs full-screen refresh (0xD7). Counter and progress bar only refresh that one strip, displays measured time for both methods on screen.
  • SLEEP: Enters deep sleep (0x10/0x01, approx. 1µA). Before entering, refreshes the entire screen to black as an "asleep" marker; after that only the MENU key can wake it (hardware reset + reinitialize), other keys are unresponsive.
  • CLEAN: Clear screen mode, fills frame buffer with white then runs one full-screen refresh waveform, screen turns all white and stays blank. LEFT / RIGHT to leave.
  • Refresh time measured: Full-screen refresh approx. 1718ms, partial refresh one strip approx. 363ms, initialization approx. 1837ms, clear screen approx. 1717ms.
  • Auto-clear ghosting: Automatically inserts one full-screen refresh every 8 partial refreshes to suppress e-ink ghosting accumulation.
  • Bottom right corner displays page number n/4, dynamic area on page fixed to one strip (Y=56, height 50), Mode / Count / PartN / Busy four items must refresh in the same strip.

06_w13_hmi​

Program Introduction

This demo program verifies the three-piece human-machine interaction: 6 buttons (MENU + joystick 5-direction), indicator LED, and buzzer. Unlike the blocking button library used by full firmware, this demo adopts fully non-blocking implementation — button scanning, LED flashing, and buzzer are all driven by main loop polling, non-blocking with each other, and not stuck by screen refresh.

On power-on, it first flashes the LED twice and beeps twice. Then the screen displays the KEYS page. Use MENU to turn pages, UP / DOWN to switch four rhythms, the LED page indicator LED changes accordingly, the BUZZER page switches four alert tone modes, press any key to hear feedback tone.

Main features include:

  • KEYS: 6 buttons real-time display of physical state (IDLE / DOWN / LONG) and short press / long press cumulative count
  • Debounce & Long Press Detection: timestamp debounce 20ms, long press threshold 800ms, after reaching the threshold generates a HOLD repeat event every 200ms
  • LED: OFF / ON / SLOW (1 Hz) / FAST (5 Hz) four rhythms, UP / DOWN to switch (long press can switch continuously), screen displays half cycle and flash count
  • BUZZER: OFF / KEY (button feedback single tone) / ALERT (long beep alert) / BEAT (beep-beep prompt tone) four modes, UP / DOWN to switch
  • Button Assignment: MENU short press to next page (KEYS page only exit, because LEFT / RIGHT need to be kept as observed objects), MENU long press to clear all counts; LEFT / RIGHT only respond to short press, avoiding long press continuous page turning
  • Any Key Feedback: In BUZZER = KEY mode, any button gives a short feedback tone (short press 40ms / long press 80ms)
  • Power-on Default: LED = SLOW (1 Hz running indicator), BUZZER = KEY; before screen initialization, flash LED twice + two short beeps
  • Buzzer uses hardware PWM drive (base frequency 2731 Hz), no CPU timer occupied

07_w13_bat​

Program Introduction

This demo program covers the complete battery chain flow: charging status read, voltage & power conversion, over-voltage/under-voltage protection, and controllability verification of the full board power domains.

When USB is plugged in, the CHG page shows charging, changes to fully charged when done; unplugging USB shows not charging. The BATT page displays voltage within tens of mV of the multimeter measured value, power slowly decreases as it discharges; PROT page switches to SIM mode, use UP / DOWN to adjust simulated voltage across thresholds, can observe protection state trigger and recovery; PWR page press PRESS to hear feedback of Grove 5V domain on/off, the other three domains marked as FIX (not disconnectable).

Main features include:

  • CHG Charging Status: displays three states — charging / fully charged / not charging, with pin level and state change count
  • BATT Voltage & Power: ADC raw code, voltage divider ratio, converted voltage, curve interpolated power, and draws discharge curve chart
  • PROT Over/Under Voltage Protection: hysteresis protection state machine, supports REAL (measured voltage) and SIM (simulated voltage) two modes, PRESS to switch
  • PWR Power Domain Management: lists controllability and current state of four power domains, PRESS to truly cut Grove 5V domain on/off, and gives whole board power consumption estimate
  • Sampling Power-off: voltage divider chain has high-side P-MOS switch (Q8), BAT_ADC_CTR set high to connect, immediately pull low after sampling to avoid continuously consuming about 200µA
  • Charging Status Correct Identification: CN3165 is open-drain output, pulled up to CHG_IND_VCC via LED + 10kΩ. This rail only has power when USB is plugged in; so it must be configured as INPUT_PULLUP to distinguish "not charging" from "floating".
  • Power Non-linear Conversion: uses 12-point lithium battery discharge curve lookup table + piecewise interpolation, because 3.7–3.9V plateau region occupies about 50% capacity, linear conversion would be severely distorted.
  • Protection Hysteresis: over-voltage 4250mV trigger / 4150mV recovery, under-voltage 3300mV trigger / 3450mV recovery, avoids repeated switching at critical points.
  • Power Domain Truthfully Labeled: only Grove 5V domain on the whole board can truly be powered off; the three 3V3 domains LoRa / GNSS / Display are always powered by three-pin LDO without EN, can only rely on chip-level sleep, page clearly marks CUT / FIX.

08_w13_sensor​

Program Introduction

This demo program collects data from two I²C sensors: BMP388 (pressure + temperature) and SHT41 (temperature + humidity), both sharing the same TWIM bus.

On the PROBE page, all five items OK means both chips' communication and calibration coefficient reading are normal. The BMP388 / SHT41 page values refresh once per second, last digit stable and non-jittering. The TREND page line chart slowly extends as pressure changes, you can see obvious trend by lightly pressing the sensor or changing altitude.

Main features include:

  • PROBE Initialization Results: I²C bus scan, BMP388 actual address and CHIP_ID, whether 21-byte calibration coefficients were read, SHT41 soft reset detection, single sampling time
  • BMP388 Pressure & Temperature: temperature, pressure, altitude converted by standard atmosphere, and two 24-bit raw ADC codes (uncompensated), can be compared with compensated results
  • SHT41 Temp/Humidity: temperature, relative humidity, dew point derived from temp/humidity (Magnus-Tetens formula), two CRC8 check results, two 16-bit raw codes
  • TREND Pressure Trend: pressure line chart of last 48 samplings, with current value / min value / max value attached
  • Dual CRC Check: SHT41's temp and humidity each carry a CRC8 (polynomial 0x31, initial value 0xFF), both must pass check to trust the data

09_w13_factory​

Program Introduction

This program is the factory comprehensive inspection firmware for W13. Based on LVGL 8.4 and hardware SPI (SPIM3 @ 32 MHz), it implements a three-page graphical interface, connecting the board's storage, display, sensors, positioning, RF, and power supply into a complete production line inspection flow: power-on self-test item by item → sensor data page → RF transceiver pair test.

Main features include:
