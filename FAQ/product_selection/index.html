Product Selection & Compatibility

Compare Meshnology models and choose the appropriate device for your application.

Which Meshnology device should I choose?​

Applicable models: N30, N32, N33, N35, N36, N37, N37E, N39, ThinkNode M9

The right device depends on your intended application.

  • N30: A compact Meshtastic device with a 1100mAh battery, suitable for beginners and everyday communication.

  • N32 / N33: Heltec V3-based devices with 3000mAh batteries, suitable for portable messaging and mesh networking.

  • N35 / N35 Plus: Devices with 3000mAh batteries and Heltec V3 or V4 hardware options.

  • N36: An nRF52840-based device designed for low-power wireless communication.

  • N37: A device featuring GPS and an OLED display.

  • N37E: A GPS-enabled device featuring a low-power E-Ink display.

  • N39: A Heltec V4-based device featuring GPS and hardware expansion capabilities.

  • ThinkNode M9: A standalone communication device with a physical keyboard, display, and GPS.

Choose your device based on battery capacity, display type, GPS requirements, firmware compatibility, and intended use.

What are the main differences between the N30, N32, N33, and N35?​

Applicable models: N30, N32, N33, N35, N35 Plus

The N30 features a 1100mAh battery, making it a compact option for portable communication.

The N32 and N33 both use the Heltec V3 platform and feature 3000mAh batteries, but they have different enclosure designs.

The N35 is available with Heltec V3 or V4 hardware options and includes a 3000mAh battery.

Please check the selected product variant and package contents before purchasing.

Can I upgrade my N35 from Heltec V3 to V4?​

Applicable models: N35, N35 Plus

The N35 enclosure is designed to accommodate compatible Heltec V3 and V4 boards.

Before upgrading, check the enclosure fit, mounting points, battery connector, and antenna connection.

Heltec V3 and V4 boards require their respective firmware targets. Always select the firmware that matches your actual hardware version.

What is the difference between N37 and N37E?​

Applicable models: N37, N37E

Both devices are based on the Seeed Wio Tracker L1 platform and feature an nRF52840 processor, SX1262 LoRa radio, and GNSS positioning.

The N37 features a 1.3-inch OLED display, while the N37E uses a 2.13-inch E-Ink display.

The OLED display is suitable for regular screen updates, while the E-Ink display is designed for low-power applications involving primarily static information.

The two models require firmware that matches their respective display configurations.

Does the Meshnology N39 have GPS?​

Applicable models: N39

Yes. The Meshnology N39 is equipped with GPS functionality and is based on the Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V4 platform.

With compatible firmware and proper configuration, the N39 can obtain location information and share its position with other nodes over a Meshtastic network.

GPS reception depends on satellite visibility and environmental conditions. For better positioning performance, use the device in an open outdoor area.

Can the N37, N37E, and N39 provide GPS tracking without cellular service?​

Applicable models: N37, N37E, N39

Yes. All three models support GPS positioning without requiring cellular service or an internet connection.

When configured with compatible Meshtastic firmware, they can share location information with other nodes through LoRa communication.

To view another device鈥檚 position remotely, a functioning radio connection must be available, either directly or through compatible mesh nodes.

GPS positioning and LoRa communication are separate functions. Obtaining a GPS fix does not automatically guarantee that another node can receive the location information.

Can I use the ThinkNode M9 without a smartphone?​

Applicable models: ThinkNode M9

Yes. The ThinkNode M9 features a physical keyboard and a 2.4-inch LCD display, allowing users to compose and read messages directly on the device.

It also features GPS, a magnetic compass, an LR1110 LoRa radio, and a 2300mAh battery.

The M9 is suitable for users who want a standalone communication device without relying on a smartphone for everyday messaging.

Please select the appropriate firmware and regional frequency version when purchasing.

  • ThinkNode M9

Does the ThinkNode M7 support both Meshtastic and LoRaWAN?​

Applicable models: ThinkNode M7

The ThinkNode M7 is available in separate Meshtastic and LoRaWAN versions.

The Meshtastic version is designed for mesh networking, while the LoRaWAN version is intended for LoRaWAN applications. These two systems cannot operate simultaneously on the same device.

The M7 features Ethernet and PoE connectivity, making it suitable for compatible gateway applications.

Please select the appropriate firmware version and regional frequency when purchasing.

Does the ThinkNode M3 have built-in sensors?​

Applicable models: ThinkNode M3

Yes. The ThinkNode M3 includes environmental and motion-sensing capabilities, including temperature, humidity, and accelerometer sensors.

It also supports GNSS positioning and LoRa communication.

Available sensor functions, data reporting intervals, and remote monitoring capabilities depend on the installed firmware and device configuration.
